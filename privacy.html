Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how NOMIS WORKS ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile apps and games we publish ("Apps").

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Unless an App's store listing states otherwise, our Apps do not require an account and do not collect personal information such as your name, email or contacts.

Some Apps may use third-party services (for example, crash reporting, analytics, advertising or in-app purchases) that collect limited device and usage data under their own privacy policies. Where an App uses such services, they are listed in that App's Google Play Data safety section or App Store privacy details.

How we use information

Any data processed is used only to operate, maintain and improve the Apps, fix crashes, and process purchases. We do not sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

Settings and progress are generally stored locally on your device. Uninstalling an App removes this data.

Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
